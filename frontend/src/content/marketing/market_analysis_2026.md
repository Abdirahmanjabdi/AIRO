## 📈 2026 Prop Firm Market Trends

### 1. The Drawdown Squeeze
In 2026, prop firms have aggressively shifted toward automated, tick-by-tick trailing drawdown calculations. What used to be a daily reset has become an active, moving trap. Virtual margins are calculated in real-time on unrealized profits, making it mathematically easier to violate drawdown limits during high-volatility spikes.

### 2. High Skepticism & Payout Friction
The industry is experiencing a massive wave of trader skepticism. High payout denials, rule updates overnight, and slippage on demo servers have convinced traders that prop firms are not looking for partners—they are bidding on the trader's emotional failure (B-book model).

### 3. The Psychology of "Tilt"
Prop firm rules are designed to exploit human cognitive vulnerabilities:
*   **The Sunk Cost Trap:** Trying to recover a evaluation fee.
*   **Revenge Trading:** Re-entering immediately after a loss to "prove the market wrong."
*   **Sizing Surges (Lot Deviation):** Doubling size to force a quick break-even.
